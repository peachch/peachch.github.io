Biography

Hi, I am Yuxi Sun, a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Jing Ma. I received my M.Sc. in Information Technology from City University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Rosa Chan, and my B.Eng. in Automation from Central South University.

My research focuses on building trustworthy and controllable LLM agents. I am broadly interested in reliable reasoning, fact-checking, retrieval-augmented generation, graph-based retrieval, causal LLMs, multi-agent systems, uncertainty-aware abstention, hallucination mitigation, and reliable evaluation.

My work has appeared at ACL, EMNLP, IJCNLP-AACL, and ICLR.

Research Interests

  • Trustworthy LLMs: reliable reasoning, uncertainty-aware abstention, hallucination mitigation, and trustworthy evaluation
  • Fact-Checking & RAG: evidence-grounded fact verification, retrieval-augmented generation, and graph-based retrieval
  • Causal LLMs: causality-inspired methods for understanding, evaluating, and improving LLM reasoning
  • Multi-Agent Systems: collaborative LLM agents for complex reasoning, retrieval, and verification tasks
