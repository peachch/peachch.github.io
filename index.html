Biography

Hi, I am Yuxi Sun, a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Jing Ma. My research focuses on building trustworthy and controllable LLM agents, spanning intrinsic reasoning reliability, retrieval-augmented grounding, and reliable evaluation. I am particularly interested in faithful reasoning, uncertainty-aware abstention, hallucination mitigation, graph-based retrieval, and how LLMs arbitrate between parametric knowledge and retrieved evidence.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as an NLP Algorithm Engineer at TCL & Ping An and as a Research Assistant at City University of Hong Kong. My work has appeared at ACL, IJCNLP-AACL, and ICLR.

Research Interests

  • Reliable Cognition: faithful reasoning trajectories, uncertainty-aware abstention, hallucination mitigation, and explainable norm-grounded judgment
  • RAG & Graph-based Retrieval: evidence-grounded reasoning over incomplete, scattered, and heterogeneous information through structured retrieval and alignment
  • Dynamic Evaluation of Retrieved Information: diagnostic benchmarks and state-conditioned metrics for studying how LLMs arbitrate between parametric priors and retrieved context
