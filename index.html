Biography

Hi, I am Yuxi Sun , an NLP researcher at Hong Kong Baptist University. My research focuses on making large language models trustworthy — teaching them when to answer and when to abstain, and evaluating their chain-of-thought reasoning through the lens of causality.

My work has appeared at ACL and IJCNLP-AACL. Outside research, I believe in "work and play".
